Help & Tutorial

How to build an Audio Style, step by step.

Build a style in 4 steps

1. Import — open a WAV recording from Home (Import WAV) or drag & drop it into the WAV Editor.
2. Edit — trim, fade and normalise in the WAV Editor. Use the Loop Studio to detect BPM and transients, and slice the audio into beats or bars.
3. Arrange — in the Style Arranger, assign finished loops to Intro, Main, Fill In, Break and Ending sections. Each section is validated before export.
4. Export — in the Export Manager, export a genuine Yamaha Audio Style (.aus) that loads directly on the PSR-S975, or a WAV / ZIP package.

Tips

• The demo drum loop (Home → Generate Demo Drum Loop) is a good first file to practise on.
• Loops play seamlessly when their length is a whole number of beats — the Arranger shows a “whole beats” badge when it fits.
• Use the Sample Library to keep your loops organised with tags, and Find duplicates to spot copies.
• Everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

What it does

Waveform editing (trim, split, fades, gain, normalise, reverse), BPM and transient detection, seamless loop building, a 15-section Style Arranger (Intro, Main, Fill In, Break, Ending), a Sample Library, and export of genuine Yamaha Audio Styles (.aus), WAV and project ZIPs — plus an .aus Player with a full arranger engine.

Apply gain

Applies to the current selection (or the whole file if nothing is selected).

Normalise

Scales the whole file so its peak reaches the target level.

Remove silence

Removes the middle of silent gaps longer than the minimum, keeping 60 ms at each edge.
